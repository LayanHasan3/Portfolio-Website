// About Me

I'm Layan Hasan, a Computer Science student at the University of Karachi who enjoys turning ideas into working software. My work spans full-stack web apps, AI-powered tools, and automated workflows — I've built quiz generators backed by the Gemini API, wired up n8n automations that qualify leads and send emails, and put together desktop and mobile apps using core data structures like linked lists and hash maps.

I like working across the whole stack: designing interfaces in Figma, building APIs with FastAPI and React, and making sure the end result is clean and usable. Outside of code, I've studied SEO and digital marketing to round out how I think about building for real users. Always looking to pick up new tools.
